No shorts, no leverage, at most one switch a month. Rebalance the first trading day of each month, and only through a 2-point no-trade band; 10bps per unit of turnover (25 crypto, 3 FX).

A leg is held while it is above its 200-day average and its 12-1 momentum is positive; otherwise that leg is cash. Held legs are sized 1/volatility (63-day, annualised) with a 40% cap each; the rest is cash. Package — Scale. Weights stay exactly as published — the basket is rebalanced back to them, never re-shaped. Each basket's exposure is 1, 0.5 or 0, read off its own index vs its 200-day average and 12-1 momentum. Capital is split equally across the three baskets; whatever a scale leaves unspent is cash at 0%. A 10% portfolio drawdown halves every exposure until equity recovers to within 5% of the peak.

Now (2026-11-06): S&P 500 10%, Bitcoin 10%, Nasdaq Composite 7%, Gold (front month) 7% +11 more · cash 17%

Package · high yield — Gear. One sleeve over both kinds of market: the owned ones (BTC, ETH, WTI crude) and the bought leverage (S&P 500 3x, Nasdaq-100 3x, semis 3x, 20y Treasuries 3x, gold 2x) — eight instruments in five clusters: US index leverage, Treasuries, gold, crypto, crude. The leverage is a holding, not a weight above 1: a 3x fund's price already carries its financing and its daily rebalance, so the drag is measured instead of promised. A leg is bought only while it is above its own 200-day average with positive 12-1 momentum; inside a cluster only the strongest momentum leg is held, so three 3x equity funds are one position and a choice — never three bets. The risk budget is split 1/vol across the clusters that are in, capped at 40% a leg, then the sleeve is scaled so its ex-ante vol is the tier's target — 15% core, 22% balanced, 30% high yield; a 20% drawdown halves the sleeve until equity is back within 6% of the peak. Monthly rebalance through the 2-point band, 20bps per unit of turnover on a levered fund, 25bps crypto, 10bps crude; cash earns 0% and an empty sleeve holds cash.

Now (2026-11-06): Ethereum 40%, WTI crude 40% · cash 20%

Momentum — Own a leg while it is above its 200-day average with positive 12-1 momentum; size 1/vol, 40% cap. Wins in: long, one-way trends — 2020-21, 2023-24. Loses in: whipsaw: a market that crosses its average six times a year pays six round trips.

Trend & breakout — Enter a leg above its 200-day and 50-day averages when within 2% (or 5 days) of a 63-day high, and hold while above both averages; equal weight. Wins in: breakouts that keep going, and quiet starts to big moves. Loses in: a market grinding sideways just under its high — the entry is always a little late and the exit a little early.

Mean reversion — Own a leg while it is above its 200-day average but below its 20-day average (a pullback inside an uptrend), sized by depth of the dip over its own volatility. Wins in: choppy bull markets where every dip gets bought. Loses in: a real trend change: the 200-day average turns last, so the first three dips are the whole drawdown.

Low volatility — Of the legs above their 200-day average, keep the calmest half by 63-day volatility; 1/vol inside that half. Wins in: risk-off years, and any client who measures the ride and not only the return. Loses in: a melt-up led by the loudest names — this style is structurally underweight what runs.

Risk parity — Own every leg above its 200-day average, with no momentum filter at all, and split the risk 1/vol — one leg per cluster where the tier has clusters. Wins in: broad participation: the least opinionated way to be invested. Loses in: a market that is above its average and falling — it holds the leg the other four styles have already sold.

    10What you get, and what you don't — in the same breath

    What you actually get

    1. The board above, mailed — one line per tripwire: current value, your number, distance left.
    2. An alert the moment it crosses — sent within the hour, with the timestamped value and the chain hash as evidence. Delivery is a human pressing send today; wiring SMTP is the next step, and the page will say so until it is done.
    3. Weekly digest — what moved past a number, nothing that didn't.

    What you don't get

    1. Predictions. The alert says it crossed your number, never it will go up.
