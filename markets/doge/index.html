How it works

  1. Choose Dogecoin (and up to 3 other markets) and the number you care about.
  2. A plain script reads the price every hour and compares it with your number. No AI decides anything.
  3. When it crosses, you get one email. The public board's alerts are also logged with SHA-256 hashes in the alert log, so their time and value can be checked later.
