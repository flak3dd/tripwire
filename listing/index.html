Title

Tripwire — hourly price & FX alerts on your number, not a pundit's

Subtitle (one line)

You pick the number. It emails you within the hour that the market crosses it. No charts, no

forecasts, no Discord.

Description

Every market email you get is an opinion. Tripwire is a comparison.

You set a number — "tell me if Bitcoin goes under A$100k", "tell me if AUD/USD gets above 0.72".

Every hour a job fetches the live value, compares it to your number, and if it has crossed, you get

an email with the timestamped reading attached as evidence. If it hasn't crossed, you hear nothing

until the weekly digest.

What you get

  • The four tripwires currently running: BTC, ETH, SOL in AUD, plus AUD/USD.
  • Hourly checks; alert sent within the hour of a cross.
  • A weekly digest that lists what moved past a number this week and how close the rest are.
  • Every alert carries the value and timestamp it fired on, so you can check it yourself.

What you don't get

  • Predictions. The alert says it crossed your number — never it will go up.
  • Charts, dashboards, mobile apps, a community, or a course.
  • Made-up social proof. This is a new product; the subscriber count is however many people asked,

and right now that is a small number.

Actually useful if you hold crypto in AUD and want a tripwire without checking charts, or you're a

small importer watching AUD/USD before you quote a job.
