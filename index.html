The four things an Australian already owns, in the weights an Australian already owns them: two banks for the earnings that pay the dividend, BHP for the commodity cycle, and the index for everything else.

Research note — why these weights
  • Banks are 50 points because Australian bank earnings move with the cash rate and housing credit, not with US tech sentiment — the two banks are half the basket and today's combined move is mostly theirs.
  • BHP at 25 is the commodity leg: iron ore and copper set its revenue, so this basket already contains a China-growth bet, sold to you as one stock.
  • The ASX 200 leg is 25 points of diversification for free — it holds the other three at slightly different weights, so a single-name blow-up cannot take the basket alone.
  • The honest risk is concentration by geography: four Australian exposures means a domestic recession is a basket-wide event, not one holding's problem.

What would invalidate it: A sustained fall in the cash rate that compresses bank net interest margins at the same time the iron ore price falls — both legs down for one macro reason, which is exactly when a 50-point bank weight stops diversifying.

The two things people actually argue about — US earnings and the price of safety — in one number: 80 points of US equity in three shapes, against 20 points of gold as the thing that is not equity.

Research note — why these weights
  • The index legs (50) and the single names (30) overlap by construction: Apple and Microsoft are among the largest weights inside both the S&P 500 and the Nasdaq, so real single-name exposure is higher than the 30 points shown. That is stated here rather than hidden behind a label.
  • Gold at 20 is the only holding whose price is not somebody's earnings — it moves on real yields and reserve demand, so it is the leg that can carry the basket on a day US equity does not.
  • FX is inside this basket whether you like it or not: three holdings quote in USD, so an Australian holder is also long USD against AUD. Weights act on returns, so this basket does not pretend the currency is neutral.
  • No bonds, no cash leg. The basket is always fully invested, which means its only defence is the 20-point gold position.

What would invalidate it: A US earnings recession with gold falling at the same time (real yields up, dollar up) — then 80 points of equity and the 20-point hedge go down together.
